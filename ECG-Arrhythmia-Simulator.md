# Experiment 01 — ECG Arrhythmia Simulator

**Subject:** Biomedical Instrumentation Lab  
**Department:** Biomedical Engineering, Easwari Engineering College  

---

## 🎯 Aim
To study and analyse different cardiac arrhythmias using the ECG 
Arrhythmia Simulator and observe their waveform characteristics.

---

## 📋 Apparatus Required
- ECG Arrhythmia Simulator
- Cathode Ray Oscilloscope (CRO)
- Connecting leads
- ECG graph paper

---

## 📖 Theory
An ECG (Electrocardiogram) records the electrical activity of the heart.
A normal ECG consists of:
- **P wave** — atrial depolarisation
- **QRS complex** — ventricular depolarisation
- **T wave** — ventricular repolarisation

### Common Arrhythmias Studied:
| Arrhythmia | Characteristic |
|------------|----------------|
| Normal Sinus Rhythm | Regular P-QRS-T, HR 60–100 bpm |
| Tachycardia | Heart rate > 100 bpm |
| Bradycardia | Heart rate < 60 bpm |
| Atrial Fibrillation | Irregular rhythm, no clear P wave |
| Ventricular Fibrillation | Chaotic, life-threatening rhythm |

---

## 🔬 Procedure
1. Connect the ECG simulator to the CRO using leads
2. Set simulator to Normal Sinus Rhythm
3. Observe and record the waveform
4. Switch to each arrhythmia mode one by one
5. Record the waveform characteristics for each

---

## 📊 Observations

| Arrhythmia | Heart Rate (bpm) | P Wave | QRS | Rhythm |
|------------|-----------------|--------|-----|--------|
| Normal Sinus | 75 | Present | Normal | Regular |
| Tachycardia | 120 | Present | Normal | Regular |
| Bradycardia | 45 | Present | Normal | Regular |
| Atrial Fibrillation | Irregular | Absent | Normal | Irregular |
| Ventricular Fibrillation | — | Absent | Chaotic | Chaotic |

---

## ✅ Result
Successfully observed and analysed different cardiac arrhythmias using 
the ECG Arrhythmia Simulator. Each arrhythmia showed distinct waveform 
characteristics as expected theoretically.

---

## 📌 Clinical Significance
ECG arrhythmia analysis is critical in cardiology for diagnosing 
life-threatening conditions. Biomedical engineers design the instruments 
and algorithms that detect these patterns automatically.
